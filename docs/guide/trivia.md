# Study: trivia

- The app's first commit is also its biggest ever: a 7,166-line scaffold
  copied from the platform's own app template (`78ba692`, 2026-08-17).
- Splitting a card into a fact and the question asked of it, adding the
  difficulty-drift rating, and building the one-file import/export and
  agent brief were three separate commits. All three landed within
  thirty-two minutes of each other on the same Sunday evening (`86c7a9c`,
  `cd16ad6`, `88693de`, all 2026-08-23).
- Its longest commit subject is a warning about its own alerting going off
  for the wrong reason: "ci: continue-on-error on alert-close tidy-up so
  it can't page @here over a green update (P6.7)" (`9d02e21`, 2026-09-07).
- Its shortest subject is "docs: build handoff brief" (`53efc9d`,
  2026-08-17) — written the same day the repo itself was created.
- Almost the entire project runs on ordinary daytime hours: in three
  months of history, only one commit by a person landed between 1 a.m.
  and 5 a.m. (`a9000f8`, 2026-11-01, 01:52).
